Health Onward’s Mission

Health Onward was created by Kiersten to help young people learn to take an active role in their own healthcare as they transition into adulthood.

The idea grew from her own experience. As a young teenager, Kiersten was misdiagnosed and had to learn how to speak up, ask questions, and become an active participant in her healthcare. Through that experience, she learned an important lesson: knowing how to advocate for your health is a life skill.

As teenagers become young adults, they begin taking responsibility for things their parents may have previously managed, including knowing their medical history and medications, preparing for appointments, asking questions, understanding what their providers tell them, and following through on next steps.

Kiersten created Health Onward to help make that transition easier by giving young people a simple place to organize their health information, prepare for appointments, keep track of what happens, and develop the skills to advocate for themselves.
